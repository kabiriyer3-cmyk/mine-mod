# In-game test checklist, 0.4.0-rc1

1. Creative world, superflat or plains. Place the silo on grass: pad sits flush, 0.25 above ground, hatch closed. Look from the side: no floating parts.
2. Place on sand over water / on bedrock / next to a chest below: placement refused with a message.
3. Load a missile: latch sound, hatch stays closed. Get the designator (empty-hand use).
4. Hold use on a point 100–500 blocks away: crosshair turns green, 0.5 s lock, hatch opens with hydraulics, booster lights in the shaft (smoke boils out, dust ring), missile leaves vertically, rolls, fins pop out, booster drops and tumbles to the ground, wings swing out.
5. Keep holding and sweep the laser: missile follows. Release: it continues to the last point.
6. Impact: fireball, dark smoke, dust skirt, dirt chunks, crater of real blocks with scorched earth. No vanilla explosion particles or sounds.
7. Hatch closes about 5 s after launch. Reload, launch again.
8. Sneak + hit the silo after the cycle: item back, shaft column refilled.
9. Check the designator in first person, third person (F5), inventory, item frame, on the ground. Report which views look wrong (screenshots please).
10. Dedicated server + second client: second player sees launch, flight and impact.

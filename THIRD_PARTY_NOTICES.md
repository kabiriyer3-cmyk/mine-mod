## Fonts used to rasterize labels

The optional asset generator uses DejaVu Sans / DejaVu Sans Mono to rasterize labels into original texture images. Font binaries are not bundled. DejaVu is based on Bitstream Vera and includes public-domain DejaVu changes. See `docs/DEJAVU_LICENSE.txt` for the installed font package's license notices.

Minecraft is a separately obtained game and is not bundled. Fabric Loader, Fabric API, Yarn, Loom, Gradle, Java, JUnit, Gson, JOML and Mixin retain their respective licenses. The build resolves dependencies in the normal way; their binaries are not embedded in this source ZIP.
